Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DreamOnGames ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with our website and our use of the Pinterest API. We are an independent studio based in Türkiye that creates printable bingo and party games, sold through our Etsy shop.

1. Summary

  • Our website does not ask for, collect or store any personal information from visitors.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or contact forms on this website.
  • Our Pinterest integration is used only to publish and manage Pins on our own Pinterest account.
  • We never sell, rent or share personal information.

2. Information we collect on this website

None. You can browse this website without providing any personal information. We do not set cookies and we do not run analytics or tracking scripts.

3. Hosting and technical data

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and our domain is managed through Cloudflare. Like most web services, these providers may automatically process technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit, for the purpose of delivering the website securely and protecting it against abuse. This processing is carried out by those providers under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

We do not access or use this technical data to identify individual visitors.

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when delivering font files. See the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Our use of the Pinterest API

We use the Pinterest API to automate publishing Pins about our own products (for example, product images and videos) to our own Pinterest account. In connection with this:

  • We access only our own Pinterest account, with our own authorization.
  • We do not access, collect or store data about other Pinterest users, their boards, Pins or profiles.
  • The access tokens issued by Pinterest are kept private and used only by our own publishing tools.
  • We do not sell, share or transfer any data obtained through the Pinterest API to third parties.
  • We use the Pinterest API in accordance with Pinterest's Developer Guidelines and Terms of Service.

If you interact with our Pins on Pinterest, that activity is governed by the Pinterest Privacy Policy.

5. Third-party links

Our website links to our Etsy shop and our Pinterest profile. Purchases are processed by Etsy, and any information you provide there is handled under the Etsy Privacy Policy. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

6. Children's privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal information through this website, we generally hold no personal data about our visitors. If you believe we hold information about you, you may contact us to request access, correction or deletion, and we will respond in line with applicable law, including the GDPR and Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK).

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if we add new features such as an online store. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us through our Etsy shop messages.
