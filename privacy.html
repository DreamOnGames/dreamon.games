Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DreamOnGames ("we", "us", "our") handles information on this website and in our Pinterest publishing tool. DreamOnGames creates printable bingo and party games, sold through our Etsy shop.

In short

  • This website does not collect any personal information.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics, tracking or advertising tools.
  • Our Pinterest integration only works with our own Pinterest account.
  • We never sell, rent or share data with anyone.

1. This website

You can browse this website without giving us any personal information. It has no sign-up, contact forms, cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and all of its files (including fonts and images) are served from our own site.

2. Our Pinterest integration

We use the Pinterest API only to publish Pins about our own products to our own Pinterest account. Our tool does not access, collect or store any information about other Pinterest users. The access tokens Pinterest issues to us are kept private, used only by our own tool, and never shared, sold or transferred. We follow Pinterest's Developer Guidelines.

3. Links to other websites

This website links to our Etsy shop and our Pinterest and Instagram profiles. Purchases are handled entirely by Etsy, and your activity on those sites is covered by their own privacy policies.

4. Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

5. Changes to this policy

If we add new features in the future, such as an online store, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy? Send us a message through our Etsy shop.
